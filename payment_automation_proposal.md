# Payment Automation & Licensing — Project Proposal

**Prepared for:** Manager Review
**Date:** 2026-05-27
**Project:** ACH & Licensing Automation via Chase API

---

## Overview

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Finance Team, Support Team |
| **What** | Automate ACH payment collection and licensing management |
| **How** | Chase API + Stripe integration to streamline payment and licensing workflows |
| **Impact** | Reduce manual effort, eliminate revenue leakage, automate billing |

---

## Problem Statement

The current payment and licensing process is entirely manual:

- Support team collects payment information in **offline mode**
- Finance team manually updates payment records
- Licensing information is updated and maintained by hand
- This leads to **revenue leakage** and **lost revenue** due to delays and human error

---

## Proposed Solution

Automate the end-to-end payment and licensing workflow using:

- **Chase API** — for ACH bank account collection and processing
- **Stripe** — for card-based payment collection
- A streamlined payment maintenance mode to reduce back-and-forth between teams

---

## Expected Impact

| Area | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Support Team | Significantly less time spent collecting ACH and payment info manually |
| Finance Team | Automated billing and invoice generation — no manual entry |
| Business | Reduced revenue leakage from delayed or missed license updates |
| Operations | Single streamlined payment maintenance flow across both teams |

---

## Current Scope

The initial phase focuses on the **Dezi flow**:

1. Automate ACH collection via Chase API
2. Automate Stripe payment collection
3. Automate licensing flow tied to payment status
4. Streamline payment maintenance mode for ongoing account management

---

## Future Scope

Planned phases after initial rollout:

1. **Partner Payment Reconciliation** — automated matching of partner payments
2. **Reseller Payouts** — ability to pay resellers and generate payout reports
3. **Marketing & Product Services** — expose additional product offerings within the payment flow
